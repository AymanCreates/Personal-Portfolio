AboutIstanbul — {{ clockLabel }}

Twelve years of putting the craft before the pitch.

I'm Mohamed — a Regional Art Director working across brand, editorial and digital product. I've spent the last decade building identities and the systems that keep them alive long after the launch deck is closed.

Most of my work starts in language, not shape. What is this thing refusing to be? Answer that honestly and the mark, the grid and the motion usually follow without much argument.

I've led creative across four countries — agency floors in Dubai, in-house teams in Istanbul, studio work from Gaza to Germany. Different rooms, same habit: build fewer things, finish them properly.

These days I take on a small number of engagements a year, usually as a lead embedded with an existing team.
